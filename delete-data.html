Delete your data

Application: Chronostone (com.SP.gemquest)
Last updated: 27 October 2026

Chronostone does not use accounts of its own — there is no username, email address, or password associated with your play (signing in with Google Play Games is optional). You can delete everything the Game holds about you from inside the Game itself, without contacting anyone.

Deleting your data from inside the Game

  1. Open Chronostone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Other Settings.
  4. Tap Delete My Data.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately: the Game itself deletes the server-side records and then clears your device. No manual request and no waiting period are involved.

This cannot be undone. Deleting your data also removes the record of any purchases you have made, which means they can no longer be restored. If you reinstall the Game afterwards, you start over as a new player.

What gets deleted

Purchase entitlement records Unity Gaming Services The in-game button
Game progress and settings On your device The in-game button, or simply uninstalling the Game
Progress backup (levels, stars, currency), keyed by the device's Android ID Our server (Cloudflare) The in-game button

Anonymous play statistics On your device, and on our server (Cloudflare) once sent; the server records the country the upload came from, never the IP address The in-game button — deletes the local copy and discards the random install identifier, so anything already on the server can no longer be associated with your installation

What is not covered

Two categories of record are outside our control:

  • Google Play transaction records. When you pay, Google — not us — records the transaction, and keeps it under Google's own retention policy. We never see your payment details. To ask about data Google holds, contact Google Play support.
  • Records we are required to keep by law, such as for tax or accounting purposes. These contain no personal identifiers from the Game.

If you cannot open the Game

If you have already uninstalled Chronostone, the data stored on your device is gone with it. Any remaining server-side record is an anonymous identifier with no way to link it to you — we would have no means of identifying which record is yours, so there is nothing we could act on.

If you have a question we have not covered here, email spstudios.play@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

Retention after deletion

Deleted records are removed from active systems immediately. Backup copies are rotated out within 90 days, after which no trace remains.
